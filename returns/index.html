Returns & Refunds

This policy applies to Name It Studio orders delivered in India.

Made-to-order nameplates

We accept returns for defective products, including nameplates that arrive damaged. Personalised products made to the approved design are ineligible for change-of-mind returns. We do not offer exchanges. Your rights under applicable consumer law still apply, including if your order differs from what was agreed.

Report an issue with your order

Email info@nameitstudio.in or call +91 90824 05720. Include your order details and a brief description of the issue. If available, photos of the product and packaging can help us understand what happened.

Return shipping and refunds

There is no return shipping charge for eligible returns. Contact us before sending the product back so we can confirm the return arrangements. If a refund is due, we’ll confirm the amount, payment method and expected timing with you.
