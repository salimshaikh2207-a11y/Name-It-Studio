Choose the material before the lighting

LED lighting can be requested with embossed acrylic, glass, engraved clear cast acrylic and charcoal nameplates. These materials create different visual effects, from the lighter appearance of clear acrylic to a darker charcoal centre. Debossed acrylic does not include an LED option.

The studio offers warm white, cool white and an illustrative RGB preview. Use the day and evening controls to compare the effect. The final lighting system, colour and construction are confirmed with your quote.

Glass lettering and decorative backdrops

Glass nameplates are offered in black or white, with frosted lettering or raised acrylic letters. A gold or silver diamond backdrop is available only with frosted letters on glass. The configurator shows these options only when they apply.

For raised lettering, the standard metallic choices are gold, silver and rose gold. Ask about any special colour or logo detail in your enquiry so it can be considered alongside the artwork and lighting.

Charcoal borders shape the finished piece

Charcoal designs pair a darker centre with an engraved acrylic border and raised letters. The outline follows the chosen border design: selecting a different decorative border can change the silhouette. The shape is therefore tied to the design rather than freely interchangeable.

Engraved clear cast acrylic and charcoal collections start at 12 × 6 inches, with 16 × 8 and 24 × 8 options. The smaller 8 × 6 option is not offered in these collections. Custom dimensions can be requested for review.

Plan the location with us

Tell us where the nameplate will be placed and include a photo of the space if useful. Outdoor exposure, power supply, installation and mounting requirements should be discussed as part of your quote; the preview does not establish suitability for a particular location.

The live preview is a design concept, not a CNC or manufacturing file. Once you have chosen your direction, send the brief and we will confirm the artwork, dimensions, lighting and price before production.
