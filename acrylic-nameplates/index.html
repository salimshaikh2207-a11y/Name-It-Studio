Debossed or embossed: choose the finish

Debossed acrylic pairs a laser-cut front sheet with a contrasting backing sheet. The lettering reads through the cut-out design, making the two colours part of the same composition. This is an unlit nameplate, so there is no LED option.

Embossed acrylic uses raised lettering on the face of the plate. It is a good direction to explore when you want dimensional letters and the option of a backlit effect. Start by comparing both techniques in the live studio.

Choose a palette that belongs at your entrance

Standard plate options are black, white and matte black, paired with gold, silver or rose-gold lettering or backing. If you have a different colour in mind, include it in your enquiry so we can confirm an appropriate finish.

Rectangle, round and capsule shapes are available for applicable acrylic designs. Each design has a fixed border, adapted to the selected shape. Round designs use equal width and height, with the final dimensions confirmed in your quote.

Make the text readable

Add your family name, individual names, business name or main text, followed by an optional house or flat number. Choose from five fonts each for English, Hindi and Marathi. Enter your own text in the chosen language; the studio does not translate it.

The on-screen preview helps you compare the composition. The final lettering is checked against the minimum one-inch finished letter height, and the artwork is refined before production.

From preview to a personal quote

Choose material, shape, border, size, text and finish in the nameplate studio. Standard acrylic size options include 8 × 6, 12 × 6, 16 × 8 and 24 × 8 inches, with custom dimensions by enquiry.

Send your design brief on WhatsApp and attach any reference image there. We prepare four nameplate artwork variants for your selection and agree the final design, price and production details with you. For a lit design, see our LED nameplate guide.
