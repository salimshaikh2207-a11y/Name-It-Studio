FLATS & APARTMENTS

Choose the size around your available entrance space.

For flat and apartment entrances, measure the usable wall or door area before choosing a design. The visual balance matters as much as the overall dimensions, especially when the nameplate includes a family name and flat number.

Compact entrances

Use a simpler layout with clear lettering and a readable flat number. Compact designs work well where the wall space is narrow.

Medium wall spaces

Allow more room for a border, icon, family name or layered lettering without making the entrance feel crowded.

Statement entrances

Larger wall areas can accommodate decorative borders, LED lighting or a more detailed custom composition.

HOME NAMEPLATE FAQ

Questions before you choose.

What name plate works best for a flat or apartment entrance?

The best choice depends on your available wall or door space, preferred material and whether you want lighting. Acrylic and compact LED designs are common choices for flat and apartment entrances.

Can I add my family name and house or flat number?

Yes. Family names, individual names and house or flat numbers can be personalised into the final artwork.

Can I order a custom size?

Yes. Standard sizes are available for many designs, and custom dimensions can be discussed where the selected construction allows it.

Can the nameplate be made in Marathi or Hindi?

Yes. English, Hindi and Marathi text can be used where suitable for the chosen design and font.

Do you show the design before production?

Yes. The artwork is finalised with you before production begins.
