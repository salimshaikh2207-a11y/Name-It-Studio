BULK & RESELLER FAQ

Before you enquire.

Do you offer bulk pricing?

Yes. Bulk pricing is quotation-based and depends on product type, size, material, personalisation and quantity.

Can resellers partner with Name It Studio?

Yes. Resellers can enquire about repeat supply and trade pricing. Partnership terms depend on expected volume and product mix.

Can one design be repeated with different names or house numbers?

Yes. Standardised designs can be produced with changing names or numbers, subject to artwork and production feasibility.

Do you work with builders, interior designers and businesses?

Yes. We accept enquiries from builders, interior professionals, offices, businesses and other trade customers for bulk and repeat orders.
