Privacy & Enquiries

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Name It Studio is a Mumbai-based custom nameplate business. Contact us at info@nameitstudio.in or +91 90824 05720 about your information.

Information you share

When you request a quote, you may provide your name, phone number, design preferences, house number, measurements and comments. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed for your nameplate order.

How the website works

Our current website quote forms open a prepared WhatsApp message. Completing the form alone does not submit your details to a Name It Studio database. Your message reaches us only when you send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

What we use enquiries for

We use information you send to respond to your enquiry, prepare artwork and quotes, manage your order, arrange delivery, and follow up about that enquiry. We do not promise that an enquiry form alone creates an order.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics and may use Google Ads measurement to understand page visits and interactions such as WhatsApp-link clicks. These signals are not proof of a completed WhatsApp conversation or sale. You can manage analytics-related cookies through your browser settings.

Contact and changes

For questions, correction or deletion requests concerning information you have shared with us, email info@nameitstudio.in. We will review requests in accordance with applicable requirements. This notice will be updated when a separate lead-storage system is enabled.
