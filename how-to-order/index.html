OPTION 01 / PICK A PRODUCT

Order from the catalogue.

  1. Choose a design. Browse the catalogue and filter by collection or lighting. Check the size, price and product details.
  2. Make it personal. Select “Choose this item”. Add the name, house number or wording you want. Use the listed size or choose a custom size; quantity and special requests are optional.
  3. Continue to WhatsApp. Your product details and personalisation are prepared automatically. If you are unsure about size or material, tell us and we’ll recommend an option.
Browse the catalogue ↗

OPTION 02 / YOUR OWN IDEA

Request a custom piece.

  1. Tell us what you imagine. Open the custom-project form and choose the item category, design direction, material and lighting.
  2. Add the details. Describe your idea. Add dimensions if you know them, quantity and any notes. You can preview a reference image in the form.
  3. Share it on WhatsApp. Review your brief and continue to WhatsApp. Send the message and attach your photo, sketch, logo or QR code separately; the image preview is not uploaded automatically.
Request a custom item ↗

Then we make it yours.

  1. Receive 4 design options. We prepare personalised artwork directions from your selected design or brief so you can compare and choose.
  2. Approve the final artwork and price. We refine your chosen direction, confirm size, finish, final price and delivery details.
  3. Pay 30% in advance. After design approval, we share the payment details on WhatsApp and confirm the order for production.
  4. Receive your piece and pay the balance. Delivery is within 3–5 working days from order confirmation. Pay the remaining 70% on delivery.

Example: If your confirmed order total is ₹1,400, pay ₹420 after design finalisation and ₹980 on delivery.
