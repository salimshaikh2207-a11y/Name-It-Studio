A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Choosing your custom nameplate.

Name It Studio is based in Mumbai. Start with a nameplate preview or share a reference for a sign, wall piece or personal gift.

How much does a custom nameplate cost?

Our starting price is ₹999, with a limited-time ₹100 discount bringing it to ₹899. Your final quote depends on size, material, lettering, border and lighting. We confirm the design and price before production.

Can I choose English, Hindi or Marathi lettering?

Yes. The nameplate studio offers five font choices for each language. Enter your text exactly as you want it made; choosing a language does not translate it. We check readability and the minimum finished letter height when preparing your final artwork.

Which nameplates can have LED lighting?

Embossed acrylic, glass, engraved clear cast acrylic and charcoal designs can be requested with LED lighting. Debossed acrylic is an unlit, two-layer design. Explore the LED nameplate guide to compare options.

Can you make a design from my image or logo?

Share your image, sketch or idea through WhatsApp, or build a custom project brief. Reference images guide the design; the final artwork, material and production feasibility are agreed with you before you order.

How do I order, and when will it be ready?

Configure your design and send the brief on WhatsApp. We prepare four nameplate artwork variants for you to choose from, then confirm the artwork and quote. Once your design is finalised, pay 10% in advance. Pay the remaining 90% on delivery. We deliver within 3–5 working days from order confirmation. Watch our video and step-by-step guide.
