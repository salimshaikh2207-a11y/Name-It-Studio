How much does a nameplate cost?

Acrylic: ₹12/sq in without LED, ₹15 with LED. Glass: ₹12 without LED, ₹15 with LED. Charcoal with engraved design: ₹16 without LED, ₹18 with LED. Final pricing is confirmed for your approved size and construction.

Can I choose the language and size?

Yes. English, Hindi and Marathi lettering, custom sizes and material-appropriate finishes are discussed before production.

When do I pay, and when will it arrive?

Pay 30% advance after design approval and 70% before or at delivery. Delivery is typically within 3–5 working days from order confirmation.

Can you work from my own idea or photo?

Yes. Send your reference image on WhatsApp and we’ll discuss how to make it.
